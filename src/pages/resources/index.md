### Member Publications

We are pleased to list these book publications by members of the ACMS. ACMS members who authored a book are shown in **bold**. Any member who would like to have a recent book listed here is welcome to contact the [webmaster](mailto:acmsonlinewebmaster@gmail.com).

<img src="/media/Introduction_to_Discrete_Mathematics_via_Logic_and_Proof-199x300.jpg" alt="Introduction to Discrete Mathematics via Logic and Proof" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Introduction to Discrete Mathematics via Logic and Proof](https://www.springer.com/us/book/9783030253578)**  
**Calvin Jongsma** (Dordt University), Springer International Publishing (2019)

This textbook introduces discrete mathematics by emphasizing the importance of reading and writing proofs. Because it begins by carefully establishing a familiarity with mathematical logic and proof, this approach suits not only a discrete mathematics course, but can also function as a transition to proof. Its unique, deductive perspective on mathematical logic provides students with the tools to more deeply understand mathematical methodology—an approach that the author has successfully classroom tested for decades.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Shaping_a_Digital_World.jpg" alt="Shaping a Digital World" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Shaping a Digital World: Faith, Culture and Computer Technology](https://www.ivpress.com/shaping-a-digital-world)**  
**Derek C. Schuurman** (Redeemer University College), InterVarsity Press (2013)  
[Companion website for instructors and students](https://cs.calvin.edu/activities/books/shaping_a_digital_world/)

Derek Schuurman provides a brief theology of technology, rooted in the Reformed tradition and oriented around the grand themes of creation, fall, redemption and new creation. Technology is not neutral, but neither is there an exclusively "Christian" form of technological production and use. Instead, Schuurman guides us to see the digital world as part of God's good creation, fallen yet redeemable according to the law of God.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/MTtEoF.jpg" alt="Mathematics Through the Eyes of Faith" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Mathematics Through the Eyes of Faith](http://www.harpercollins.ca/books/Mathematics-Through-Eyes-Faith/?isbn=9780062024473)**  
Edited by **Jim Bradley** and **Russell Howell**, HarperOne (2011)

In a new addition to the groundbreaking "Through the Eyes of Faith" series, the nation's top Christian professors approach mathematics from a Christian perspective. Co-sponsored by the Council for Christian Colleges and Universities, this book addresses the Christian student's need to bring science into accord with faith, and illustrates how math contributes to the construction of a consistent Christian worldview.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Complex_Analysis_Mathews_Howell.jpg" alt="Complex Analysis for Mathematics and Engineering" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Complex Analysis for Mathematics and Engineering, 6th ed.](http://www.jblearning.com/catalog/9781449604455/)**  
**John H. Mathews** (California State University) and **Russell W. Howell** (Westmont College), Jones & Bartlett Learning (2011)

A comprehensive, student-friendly presentation of complex analysis that strikes a balance between the pure and applied aspects of the subject. The authors appeal to students' geometric intuition to introduce fundamental concepts and lay the foundation for more rigorous development that follows.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Math_For_Liberal_Arts.jpg" alt="MATH for Liberal Arts" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[MATH for Liberal Arts](http://www.cengage.com/search/productOverview.do?N=10+4294918395&Ntk=P_EPI&Ntt=1439047022&Ntx=mode%2Bmatchallpartial)**  
**Karl Smith** (Santa Rosa Junior College), Cengage: Brooks/Cole (2011)

Developed from over 120 interviews with students and faculty members, MATH presents concepts in a visual and approachable way, ideal for combating the math anxiety often found in Liberal Arts Math students.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Revolutions_of_Geometry.jpg" alt="Revolutions of Geometry" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Revolutions of Geometry](http://www.wiley.com/WileyCDA/WileyTitle/productCd-0470167556.html)**  
**Michael O'Leary** (College of DuPage), Wiley (2010)

Depicts how to write basic proofs in various fields of geometry, exploring the general development of geometry throughout time and placing proofs into the context of geometry's history.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Calculus_Briggs_Cochran.jpg" alt="Calculus" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Calculus](http://www.pearsonhighered.com/briggscochran1einfo/index.html)**  
Bill Briggs (University of Colorado at Denver) and **Lyle Cochran** (Whitworth University), Pearson (2010)

Drawing on their decades of teaching experience, the authors created a calculus text that carries the teacher's voice beyond the classroom. Comprehensive exercise sets have received praise for their creativity, quality, and scope.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Methods_for_Euclidean_Geometry.jpg" alt="Methods for Euclidean Geometry" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Methods for Euclidean Geometry](http://www.cambridge.org/uk/catalogue/catalogue.asp?isbn=9780883857632)**  
**Owen Byer** (Eastern Mennonite University), Felix Lazebnik (University of Delaware), and **Deirdre Smeltzer** (Eastern Mennonite University), MAA (2010)

Uses a wealth of methods to solve problems in Euclidean geometry, exploring the application of a broad range of mathematical topics to the solution of Euclidean problems.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Diff_Geo_Manifs.jpg" alt="Differential Geometry of Manifolds" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Differential Geometry of Manifolds](http://www.akpeters.com/product.asp?ProdCode=4575)**  
**Stephen Lovett** (Wheaton College), A. K. Peters (2010)

Introduces students to the modern theory of manifolds, focusing on differentiable manifolds and the study of Riemannian manifolds, concluding with applications to physics.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Voltaires_Riddle.jpg" alt="Voltaire's Riddle" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Voltaire's Riddle](https://www.maa.org/EbusPPRO/Bookstore/ProductDetail/tabid/170/Default.aspx?ProductId=2295)**  
**Andy Simoson** (King College), MAA Press (2010)

This book explores Voltaire's story *Micromegas* and its underlying mathematics.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Zeta_n_Modular_Physics.jpg" alt="A Window into Zeta and Modular Physics" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[A Window into Zeta and Modular Physics](http://www.cambridge.org/catalogue/catalogue.asp?ISBN=9780521199308)**  
Edited by Klaus Kirsten (Baylor University) and **Floyd L. Williams** (University of Massachusetts, Amherst), Cambridge University Press (2010)

An introduction to various zeta functions, modular forms, and vertex operator algebras, with concrete applications to physics.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Diff_Geo_CnS.jpg" alt="Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Differential Geometry of Curves and Surfaces](http://www.akpeters.com/product.asp?ProdCode=4568)**  
Thomas Banchoff (Brown University) and **Stephen Lovett** (Wheaton College), A. K. Peters (2010)

A one-semester undergraduate text in the differential geometry of curves and surfaces, culminating with the celebrated Gauss-Bonnet Theorem and applications to spherical and hyperbolic geometry.

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Moores_Method.jpg" alt="The Moore Method" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[The Moore Method: A Pathway to Learner-Centered Instruction](http://www.amazon.com/Moore-Method-Pathway-Learner-Centered-Instruction/dp/0883851857/)**  
**Charles A. Coppin** (Lamar University), W. Ted Mahavier, E. Lee May, and G. Edgar Parker, MAA (2009)

A practical overview of the Moore Method, serving as both a how-to manual for implementing the method and an answer to the question: what is the Moore method?

<hr style="clear:both;" />

<img src="/media/Curious_Curves.jpg" alt="Curious Curves" style="float:left;margin:0 1rem 0.5rem 0;width:110px;" />

**[Curious Curves](http://www.worldscibooks.com/mathematics/7544.html)**  
Richard Darst (Colorado State University), **Judith Palagallo** (University of Akron), and **Thomas E. Price** (University of Akron), World Scientific (2009)

Self-contained and unified, this book is suitable for a topics course, capstone course, or senior seminar, with an emphasis on the geometric intuition characteristic of the study of curves.

**[Mathematics in a Postmodern Age: A Christian Perspective](http://www.amazon.com/Mathematics-Postmodern-Age-Christian-Perspective/dp/0802849105/)**  
Edited by **Jim Bradley** and **Russell Howell**, Eerdmans

Explores the nature, historical development and cultural impact of mathematics from a Christian perspective. Examines the tensions that have arisen between modern and postmodern views of mathematics, explores alternative theories of mathematical truth, and shows how a Christian perspective makes a difference.
